### 🧭 About Me

I'm a full-stack developer who builds **complete, production-grade platforms** — not just features. My focus is on hyper-local products solving real logistics and commerce problems in Ilorin/Malete, Kwara State: marketplaces, ride-hailing, delivery, and property rental systems, built end-to-end from data model to deployed UI.

I work across the stack: **React/TypeScript** and **React Native** on the frontend, **Node.js/Express/MongoDB** on the backend, with real-world integrations (payments, maps, real-time systems, background job queues) — the kind of infrastructure decisions that come from shipping products people actually use, not tutorials.

I also design for **scale**: microservice boundaries, load-balanced services, job queues (Redis/BullMQ), caching, and socket-based real-time layers — architecting systems to handle growth from day one, not bolting it on after launch.

I also take on **freelance frontend work**, building fast, polished, production-ready interfaces for clients outside my own ventures.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

**[Veloo](https://github.com/)** — Hyper-local multi-vertical platform
A marketplace, ride-hailing (Okada), dispatch, and artisan-services platform for Ilorin/Malete. Built the full stack: multi-step vendor/artisan onboarding, a role-based admin portal with 16 granular permissions, real-time support via Socket.io, a promo/discount engine, Redis-backed background job processing with BullMQ, and Paystack payment integration.
`React` `Redux` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Redis` `BullMQ` `Socket.io` `Paystack` `Cloudinary`

**[Ilogi](https://ilogiproperties.com)** — Property rental platform
A rental listings platform with animated listing pages, a two-step inspection booking flow, Paystack-verified payments, and a media-first presentation layer for property photos and video.
`React` `Framer Motion` `Node.js` `Paystack` `Cloudinary`

**GasRun** — On-demand LPG delivery startup
Founded and building an on-demand cooking gas delivery service — riders refill cylinders on-site for customers. Owns the product end-to-end: consumer app prototype, pricing model, logistics design, investor pitch deck, and go-to-market strategy for a campus-dense market.
`Product Strategy` `HTML/CSS/JS Prototyping` `Business Modeling`

**Predicto / FlexiBet** — World Cup 2026 prediction & live match visualizer
Built for the TxODDS × Superteam World Cup Hackathon. A live match visualizer with an animated SVG knockout bracket, a real-time pitch view driven by streamed match events, a TradingView-style candlestick trading chart, and Solana wallet-based authentication.
`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Solana Wallet Adapter` `MongoDB` `BullMQ` `Server-Sent Events`

**Food Order App** — Cross-platform microservice architecture
A food ordering system built as a microservice, with a React Native (Expo) mobile client. Combines Expo push notifications with a live Socket.io layer so order status updates reach users through both channels in real time.
`React Native` `Expo` `Node.js` `Express` `Socket.io` `Microservices`

**[VLUX](https://vlux.ng)** — E-commerce platform (freelance)
Product pages and storefront work for a hair products/wigs e-commerce brand, including Next.js App Router migrations and mobile-first UI.
`Next.js` `Redux` `Tailwind CSS`
