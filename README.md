### 🧭 About Me

I'm a full-stack developer who builds **complete, production-grade platforms** — not just features. I've shipped marketplaces, ride-hailing, delivery, and rental systems end-to-end, from data model to deployed UI.

**React/TypeScript** and **React Native** on the frontend. **Node.js/Express/MongoDB** on the backend. Real-world integrations — payments, maps, real-time, job queues — and systems designed to **scale**: microservices, load balancing, caching, background processing.

Also available for **freelance frontend work** — fast, polished, production-ready interfaces, worldwide.

### 🚀 Projects

**Veloo** — Multi-vertical marketplace platform
My most significant build: one platform combining a vendor marketplace, ride-hailing, delivery dispatch, and artisan services.

- **Data layer** — designed Mongoose models for stores, riders, and artisans
- **Admin portal** — role-based access with 16 granular permissions across 4 verticals
- **Background jobs** — Redis/BullMQ queue system with a shared worker/handler registry
- **Load balancing** — traffic distributed across service instances for scale
- **Real-time support** — live chat via Socket.io
- **Payments** — end-to-end Paystack integration
- **Onboarding** — multi-step flow for new vendors and artisans (React/Redux)
`React` `Redux` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Redis` `BullMQ` `Socket.io` `Paystack`
